# Cancellation Policy

## Cancel before shipment

Customers can cancel an order before the order is shipped.

## After shipment

Orders that have already been shipped cannot normally be cancelled.

## Refund after cancellation

If an order is cancelled before shipment, the eligible amount is refunded to the original payment method.

Refund processing normally takes 5-7 business days.

## Customized products

Customized products may have additional cancellation restrictions.
